Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

LazyScoutHQ is a small independent project that shares product ideas as pins. You can reach us at lazytekstai@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms or user accounts. We do not collect personal data from visitors.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you load a page. This is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

Our Pinterest application

We use a private software tool, run only by the owner of LazyScoutHQ on their own computer, to prepare and publish pins to our own Pinterest account through Pinterest's official API.

  • The tool is not offered to the public and is not used by anyone else. We do not ask other people to log in through it.
  • It accesses only our own Pinterest account, to read our boards and to create our own pins.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about other Pinterest users, including their profiles, followers or activity.
  • The access tokens that Pinterest issues to the tool are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never sent to any other party.
  • You can stop us from accessing your data at any time. As we only access our own account, no other user's data is involved.

Affiliate links

Our pins and pages may contain affiliate links, for example to Amazon. When you click one, the retailer may set cookies or collect data under its own privacy policy. We do not control this and do not receive your personal data. We only see aggregated sales or commission reports from the affiliate program.

Your rights

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom you have rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because we collect no personal data from visitors, there is normally nothing to access or delete, but you can contact us at lazytekstai@gmail.com with any request or question. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
